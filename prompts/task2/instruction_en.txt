## HOW TO GRADE
Go through these 9 steps in order:
1. Compilation: does compile_log contain an error? Is its cause clearly another label?
2. Scope: each question/function the exam requires: "attempted" or "not attempted"; is the prerequisite (if any) right? Single-question exam: write "grade the whole program".
3. Input/output: are the data read and the printed text what the statement asks for? (rely on the program run when there is one)
4. Loops and arrays/strings: are indices, stop conditions, infinite loops, array/string access right?
5. Functions: is a function called/used wrongly, or declared differently from the required prototype?
6. Logic and hard-code: conditions/formulas, assumptions about the data, hard-coded values?
7. Algorithm: are the core computation and the data structure what the statement asks for?
8. Style: whole code commented out, forbidden library/function, needlessly complex function?
9. Review: keep the 1-2 main errors (at most 3), each with 1 line of code as evidence; drop labels that break the GRADING RULES, reuse a line already counted, or are an unclear "Lỗi edge case".
